> “What is this ‘thing’ doing between me and the system… and why does it feel wrong?”

The CatalystUI Model (“the Model”) is a universal lens for identifying and exploring the fundamental principles of user-interface design. During a time when many developers choose to solve narrowly defined, specialized problems, the CatalystUI Model takes a step back to analyze user-interface design as a concept. From this perspective, we can identify common patterns across solutions, determine each approach and its limitations, and use the information collected to establish a universal framework, thereby capturing the entire interaction process between the end-user and an information system.

The format of this documentation is organized into three levels of understanding–_beginner_, _intermediate_, and _advanced_–roughly mapping to the in-depth discussions, per-topic summaries, and the high-level overviews, respectively. While we recommend reading the terminology and summary sections in full, readers are welcome to move at their own pace through the rest of the document, skipping sections they already understand.
